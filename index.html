      About the Quiz

      This is a simple Left-Right matching quiz that allows you to upload a CSV file containing questions and answers. After uploading the file, the questions will appear on the left side and the answers on the right. Your task is to match each question with the correct answer.

      Once you've selected a question and an answer, click on "Check Answers" to verify if your match is correct!

      The answers are shuffled to make the quiz more challenging!

      How to play:

      • Upload a CSV file with two columns: questions and answers.
      • Click on a question on the left and an answer on the right.
      • Click "Check Answers" to see if your selection is correct.
